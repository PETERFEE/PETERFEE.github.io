Profile picture
===============

Drop a square photo here named exactly:

  avatar.jpg

~600x600px is plenty. It appears in the Biography section inside the rotating
gradient ring. Until the file exists the site shows a "PF" monogram plus a small
caption telling you where the photo goes -- that caption disappears on its own
the moment avatar.jpg is here.

To use a .png instead, change `avatar` in data/bio.ts to "/profile/avatar.png".
